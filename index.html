Computational estimate based on a scientific model, not a laboratory measurement. Felt model: Delany–Bazley (1970) or Miki (1990). Airflow resistivity is calibrated on measured airlaid panels made from textile waste (Rubino et al., 2023). Class, αw and NRC are calculated for diffuse sound (Paris integral, extended reaction) following the ISO 11654 method from octave values; a reverberation-room measurement (ISO 354) can differ.
